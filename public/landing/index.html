What it does

Says what to do now

One thing at a time, ranked by due date, the time it really takes, and points. What is due today underneath. Nothing you have to sort.

Reads the announcements

At GCU the real instructions are posted in announcements. The app reads every one and puts what it asks for on the assignment, with the professor's own words.

Knows where you stand

Every item says when it last matched Halo and where it came from. A moved deadline moves here too. A heavy day gets a warning the night before.

How the sync works

  1. Drag the Sync Halo bookmark to your bookmarks bar (or copy it to your phone's bookmarks).
  2. Open halo.gcu.edu and log in as you always do.
  3. Click the bookmark. Your classes, assignments, grades and announcements arrive here, and you approve them before anything changes.

The bookmark runs on Halo's own page while you are logged in there. It never sees your password and nothing is stored anywhere but in your own account. On Plus, a small Chrome extension does this on its own every few hours.

Questions

Does it need my GCU password?
No, and it never asks. The bookmark works because you are already logged in to Halo in that browser.
Is this from GCU?
No. Halo+ is an independent planner made by a student. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Grand Canyon University. Halo is GCU's learning platform.
What happens to my data?
It is yours. Export everything as one file any time, or delete the account and all of it in one tap. AI features send only the text they need to the model provider and nothing is kept there.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. Add it to your Home Screen and it works like an app, notifications included. The bookmark works on a phone too; the app walks you through it.
