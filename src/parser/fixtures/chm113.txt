General Chemistry I-Lecture
CHM-113 3 Credits Sep 8 - Dec 20
Course Description
This is the first course of a two-semester introduction to chemistry intended for undergraduates pursuing
careers in the health professions and others desiring a firm foundation in chemistry. Co-Requisite: CHM-113L.
