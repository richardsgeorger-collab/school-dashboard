Halo+

Due dates, grades and search, right on Halo's own pages in Safari.

Halo+ is on in Safari. Open Halo and you'll see it.

  1. Turn on Halo+ in Safari Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Halo+ → Allow Extension. Safari → Settings → Extensions → tick Halo+.
  2. Allow it on Halo and Halo+ On the same screen, set halo.gcu.edu and haloplus.app to Allow. The first time you open Halo, click the Halo+ button in Safari's toolbar and choose Always Allow on This Website. Do the same on haloplus.app.
  3. Open Halo in Safari Your classes, due dates and grades show up on Halo's pages. Nothing to set up there.
