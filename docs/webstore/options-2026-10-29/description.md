## What changed from the pending description, and why

- The first line is the three things only Halo+ does on Halo (the research: "one calendar" alone is not special any
  more, Better Halo has one). The old first line ("makes GCU's Halo better") echoed Better Halo's name.
- Bullets are one short line each (the long ones are gone: PDFs on the page, drafts, word counts, Halo's proof; still
  in the product).
- "Behind it, a planner of your own" (three bullets) became one line, as asked ("barely mention the app").
- The account step ("make a free Halo+ account") is no longer spelled out; the welcome tab says it after install.
- Privacy: said in its own block, plainly, including the AI check on announcements (the privacy page says the same).
  The research found Better Halo and Halo Global Calendar show "will not collect your data"; this is the honest
  version for Halo+.
- Not named: Better Halo. Halo+ works alongside it (tested every loop), but naming another extension invites a policy
  question; left out.
