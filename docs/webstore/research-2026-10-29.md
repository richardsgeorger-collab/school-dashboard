# Chrome Web Store research: GCU Halo extensions and student LMS extensions (2026-10-29)

Everything here was read on **2026-10-29** from the public Chrome Web Store (logged out, US, `?hl=en`), Firefox Add-ons
(public API), Edge Add-ons (rendered in headless Chrome), the extensions' own websites and GitHub. Nothing was signed
in to, posted, uploaded or submitted. Screenshots and tiles were downloaded to the session scratchpad only to describe
them; none are in the repo. "Could not verify" is said where it applies.

## Summary: what wins in this niche

The GCU Halo niche is tiny and young. The leader, **Better Halo**, has **1,000 users** (CWS bucket; 281 in May 2026,
874 in September per third-party trackers), 5.0 from 5 ratings. **Halo Global Calendar** has 143 users, 5.0 from 5.
**Halo+** (live listing still 0.11.1) has 22 users, 5.0 from 1. Nobody has more than five ratings, so a handful of real
reviews would change the picture. Outside GCU, the extensions that win (BetterCampus/BetterCanvas 2,000,000, Tasks for
Canvas 1,000,000, Schoology Plus 200,000) use a short "Better/Plus + platform" name, a summary that names the platform,
screenshots that are mostly a giant 2-to-5-word headline beside one cropped piece of UI, and a simple wordmark tile.

Takeaways for Halo+:

1. **Search cards show the tile (275×175), the title, the domain, the star rating and the summary. They do not show the
   user count or the number of ratings.** The tile and the summary's first words do the selling on the results page.
2. **The title is not cut off.** It is one line (16 px Google Sans Medium, ellipsis), and the box is 589 px at a
   1280-wide window (558 px at 1024, 733 px at 1440+). Halo+'s current titles use about 400–420 px, and a 75-character
   mixed-case title (548 px) still fit at 1024 and up. Only very wide text (all caps) would be cut, at about 36–47
   characters. So there is room for more search words in the title.
3. **Halo+ is missing from "Halo LMS" and "Halo calendar" searches.** Better Halo and BetterHalo say "Halo LMS".
   Halo Global Calendar is #1 for "Halo calendar". Halo+ is #1 for "GCU" and "Grand Canyon University" and #2 for "Halo
   GCU", "GCU Halo" and "halo.gcu.edu". Put "Halo LMS" in the summary or the description's first line, and keep
   "Calendar" in the title (the pending 0.12.x title already has it).
4. **Verify haloplus.app in the developer dashboard.** Better Halo gets a blue verified check next to `betterhalo.app` on
   every search card and "Created by the owner of the listed website" on its page. Halo+ shows `haloplus.app` without
   the check. This is free trust in a field where every competitor looks equally small.
5. **The pending tile line "Halo, but better." reads like the competitor's name** ("better halo", whose tile says "Make
   Halo yours."). Use a tile that could only be Halo+: the Halo+ mark large, plus one concrete promise (for example
   search with ⌘K, or "what's next on every class"), with type big enough to read at 275×175.
6. **Make screenshot captions much bigger.** Every top competitor uses a headline at about 60–110 px, 2–6 words, taking
   a third to half of the frame: Better Halo "Keep track of your work.", BetterCampus "Keep track of assignments",
   Tasks for Canvas uses coloured pill callouts, and Grade Calculator for D2L uses a headline plus one line on a
   gradient. Halo+'s pending captions are about 20 px text in a thin black bar above a full-width page. Crop to the
   feature and put a large caption beside it.
7. **Lead with what only Halo+ does.** Better Halo already has an all-classes calendar, calendar export and what-if
   grades (changelog v0.8, March 2026, though not in its store text). Halo Global Calendar is a calendar plus a merged
   announcements feed, messages, syllabus and gradebook tabs. Halo+'s own features are search across every class
   (Ctrl/⌘K), what's next with the grade on every card, the rubric in place, due-in hints, check-off when you submit,
   announcements read for you (Max), and the phone app. "One calendar" is no longer a differentiator on its own.
8. **The privacy section and the account step are visible differences.** Better Halo and Halo Global Calendar show
   "will not collect or use your data". Halo+ lists personally identifiable information, personal communications and
   website content. Tasks for Canvas fell to 3.2 stars after it pushed users into a BetterCampus switch ("Better Campus
   ruins the ease of life of the extension"). Keep the on-Halo features working with as little friction as possible,
   and say plainly in the description's first lines what stays on the computer.

Also found:
- **Two name and look collisions.** Another extension called **"Halo+"** (John Aquino, 28 users, last updated May
  2024, "hook into Halo+ … for a GCU Student") shows in Better Halo's Related carousel. Halo Global Calendar's icon is a
  purple **"H+"**. Keep "for GCU" and the ring-plus mark so students do not mix them up.
- **Edge Add-ons has none of the three.** It only has Elijah Olmos's Halo Notification Service and Import Halo
  Assignments. "Grand Canyon University" returns nothing there. Edge users can install from the Chrome store, but an
  Edge listing would have that search to itself.
- **The live Halo+ store page is still the 0.11.1 listing** ("Halo+ for GCU: Every Due Date, Even in Announcements",
  updated October 26, 2026, still says "7 days of Max free" and "Plus and Max"). The 0.12.x name, description, pictures
  and tile in LISTING.md are not live yet.
- Halo+ is declared a **Trader**, so its store page shows a street address and phone number. Better Halo, Halo Global
  Calendar and the other student extensions are all "Non-trader". This is George's call to check, not a listing fix.

## 4. How Chrome Web Store search shows these (seen 2026-10-29)

Method: `https://chromewebstore.google.com/search/<query>?hl=en`, logged out, US, server HTML. It was cross-checked in
headless Chrome at 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920 wide, and title widths were measured in the page. Results can differ for
signed-in or personalised users.

**Card anatomy:** promo tile 275×175 on the left (or the icon on white if there is no tile), then the **title** (one
line, 16 px Google Sans Medium, `white-space: nowrap; text-overflow: ellipsis`), the **website domain** (with a blue
verified check if verified), the **star average** (no count), and the **summary** (up to about 2 lines). **No user count
and no rating count on the card.** The user count shows only on the detail page ("Education · 1,000 users").

**Title room:** 558 px at a 1024 window, 589 px at 1280, 733 px at 1440 and above. Measured:
"Halo+ for GCU: Calendar, Search & Due Dates on Halo" = 402 px; "Halo+ for GCU: Every Due Date, Even in Announcements"
= 419 px; a 75-character mixed-case title = 548 px, not truncated anywhere from 1024 up. 75 "W"s would be cut at 36–47
characters. In practice the 75-character store limit binds before the card does.

Observations: Halo Global Calendar does not appear for "GCU", "Halo GCU" or "GCU Halo", although its summary contains
"GCU Halo". It does appear for "Grand Canyon University" (in its disclaimer). The matching looks word-based but not
consistent, so this is observed behaviour, not a rule. Halo+ ranks above Better Halo where its title holds the query

Common patterns:
- **Names:** "Better <platform>" or "<platform> Plus/+". In the 2,000+ user group, only Tasks for Canvas puts more than
  the name in the title. Search-word titles show up mainly among small or new listings (e.g. the 71-character "Canvas
  Grades & GPA Calculator: Missing Assignments, Finals & Due Dates", 274 users). Halo+'s keyword title is fine for
  search, but the brand should come first.
- **First lines:** one line naming the platform and the feeling ("finally feel like it was built for students", "D2L,
  finally calm and clear", "Make Halo yours"), then a scannable feature list (ALL-CAPS headings or emoji bullets),
  then "built by students", then not-affiliated. Several put "Free" or "no account" in the summary (Grade Calculator
  for D2L: "Free and private."; Canvas Grades & GPA: "Free, no account, works on any school"). BetterCampus reviewers
  bring up price unprompted ("And it's free!!").
- **Screenshots:** the bigger listings caption every screenshot with very large bold text (2–6 words) and crop the UI
  around one feature. Full-page screenshots with small captions (the pending Halo+ style) are not used by any listing
  above 7,000 users except Moodle Buddy (no captions).
- **Tiles:** wordmark/logo first. A slogan of at most 5 words is optional. No UI detail, because the tile is shown at
  275×175.
- **Ratings are fragile.** Tasks for Canvas (1M users) is at 3.2 after an update pushed users into BetterCampus: "Better
  Campus is so bad why do we have to change everything", "Doesn't work anymore… very disappointed", "bring it back".
  Brightspace/D2L PLUS (4.8) answers low reviews as the developer ("please let me know via the 'Report Bug' button");
  whether Moodle Buddy (4.9) does the same was not checked.
