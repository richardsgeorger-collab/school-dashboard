# The AI features, as they stand on 2026-09-28

The problem statement for the Study rebuild (loop 110). Captured on the real backend (build dfdfa9c, Haiku through
the deployed `ai` function) with a throwaway Max-trial account on the sample term plus one CHM-113 slide deck and one
pasted lecture, and a second throwaway on Free with its trial over. Screens in `docs/screens/ai-audit/<desk|phone>-light/`,
named `NN-<scene>.png` (the desk run was captured before the `now-hero-details` scene was added, so its numbers run
one lower and its `now-exam-mode` shot missed: the phone run has it; the dark runs were captured too and dropped
from the repo, since they showed nothing the light ones did not). Referenced below by scene name. Captured with a scratchpad script that needs the
service key, so it is not in the repo; `scripts/e2e-study.mjs` is the in-repo successor for the new screens.

## Two things that are broken today, found by this audit

1. **The coach does not answer at all in production.** Every message comes back
   "adaptive thinking is not supported on this model (Anthropic returned 502.)" (`*-now-coach-answer.png`).
   `chat/client.ts` asks for adaptive thinking, `ai/gateway.ts` puts `thinking: {type: 'adaptive'}` on the wire, the
   Edge Function forwards the body untouched, and Haiku 4.5 rejects it. The e2e mock answered instead of Anthropic, so
   the tests stayed green. The AI plan's core pass (`ingest/run.ts`, class_plan and term_plan) sends the same flag on a
   forced tool call, which Anthropic also rejects. Neither has worked since the flag went in.
2. **Every AI screen has two front doors and no home.** `#/ai` (Coach · Tutor · Study kit · Practice) is linked from
   nowhere in the app; the tabs are Now, Calendar, Classes, Inbox, You. The same four features are reached from eleven
   different buttons spread over Now, the item sheet, the class page, the library, Grades and the upgrade welcome, each
   under a different name.

## What a new student meets

- **"I have a chem quiz Friday, help me study."** The only box that takes a sentence is the coach, and it fails.
  Nothing on the quiz itself says "practice"; the item sheet has "Quiz me on this" at the bottom, under the rubric.
  Study kit and Tutor are on the class page, not on the quiz.
- **"I don't understand this engineering math homework."** Open the item → scroll → "Explain this like I'm behind"
  (tutor, scoped by title). Three clicks and a scroll, if you know the button is there.
- **"Is my essay draft good enough?"** Open the item → scroll → "Check my draft" → paste → Check. Works, four steps,
  and the button reads the same as "Check my method" beside it on other items.
- **"What should I work on tonight?"** The coach. Fails.
- **Free / trial over:** `#/ai` shows "A coach and a tutor that know your classes … 108 open things to ask about" and
  a price (`*-free-ai-hub-locked`); the coach box on Now opens the same card. Nothing shows what the tools would make
  from this student's own quizzes and material.

## What the rebuild does (loop 110)

Three tools with one name each, one home, one button per thing:

- **Ask** (`#/ask`): the coach and the tutor as one chat that always knows classes, assignments, deadlines, grades,
  slides, lectures, syllabi and announcements; short answers; every answer ends with next steps as buttons.
- **Practice** (`#/practice`): pick a quiz or exam (the coming ones first) or any class; a study plan for the time left,
  a worksheet as a real document (.docx and PDF, answers on their own last page), quiz me one question at a time,
  flashcards, a one-page sheet; from the class's own material, weakest topics first.
- **Check** (`#/check`): paste or drop the work; checked against the rubric or against how the class teaches the
  method.
- **Study** tab in the main navigation: the coming quizzes and exams each with "Practice", one "Ask anything" box,
  "Check my work". Non-Max students see the same screen with their own quizzes and one way in.
- On every assignment: "Get help" (Ask, scoped) and "Check my work"; on every quiz and exam: "Practice for this";
  "Get a prompt" stays on assignments. Now offers "Practice" when a quiz or exam is within five days.
- The thinking flag is fixed at the gateway, and the e2e for the new screens runs against the deployed function.
