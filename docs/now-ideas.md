# Now, redesigned: the ideas pass

Written before building, as asked. Each idea names where it came from and why it fits a first-semester engineering
student who has never had to study, gets overwhelmed by lists, loses interest and comes back later, and is trying to
stop opening a bad LMS. Then the cut: anything that adds a click, a decision, or something to read goes, unless it
removes more than it adds.

## What the good tools understand

1. **Sunsama — the day is planned, not discovered.** You decide what today holds once, deliberately, and the app
   holds you to that instead of showing you the whole backlog every time you look. *Fit:* the hero already is the
   one decision, made for him. Keep: one thing, no queue.
2. **Sunsama — realistic capacity, said plainly.** "You planned 6h and have 3h." *Fit:* the pace line already says
   ahead/behind per class. Cut: a second capacity sentence is a second thing to read.
3. **Motion — deadlines are constraints, not a list; time is the scarce thing.** Motion schedules work into the
   gaps around fixed events. *Fit:* the scheduler and the AI term pass do this; what is missing is telling him the
   *result* as a fact: "fits before ENG-105 at 11:00" or "4h of work, ~9h free before it's due." Build as one line.
4. **Motion — deadline risk.** Motion warns when work no longer fits. *Fit:* the app has a risk line; the brief says
   stop the countdowns. Keep the fact, drop the alarm colour.
5. **Reclaim — habits and buffers around calendar events.** *Fit:* exam study sessions exist. Cut.
6. **Things — Today / Anytime / Someday, and "This Evening".** Things lets you set a thing aside without it reading
   as failure. *Fit:* exactly the missing "blocked" state: not snoozed, not skipped, waiting on something. Build
   (2a). "This Evening" as a second slot would be a list again. Cut.
7. **Things — everything has a reason to be where it is.** Things shows why a task is in Today. *Fit:* the hero's
   "picked because" line does this; the brief says replace urgency with information. Keep the reason only when it is
   a fact ("needs X first"), drop the ranking rationale.
8. **Fantastical — natural-language capture.** *Fit:* Quick capture exists. Cut.
9. **Notion — relations between records.** *Fit:* the AI plan already carries feeds-into and prerequisites. Show
   them on the hero as facts. Build (2c).
10. **Anki — retrieval beats rereading; the forgetting curve.** *Fit:* practice sets and study kits exist, but
    nothing surfaces retrieval on a quiet day. One flashcard from his own kit when Now has nothing urgent is the
    lowest-cost retrieval there is, and the empty state is the one place a card is not clutter. Build, **off by
    default** (a thing to read).
11. **Anki — "how did it go" after each card.** *Fit:* the after-done time ask is already one decision; adding a
    second is what he asked to stop. Cut.
12. **Pomodoro / Forest — a timer turns "start" into a small, bounded act.** *Fit:* someone who has never studied
    needs the first ten minutes made small. A Start button that begins a timer and, on Done, logs the real time
    *removes* the after-done question. Build: one button, one fewer decision.
13. **Duolingo — re-entry without shame.** *Fit:* Welcome back exists and says what slipped, not that he failed.
    Keep; check its tone in the fresh-eyes pass.
14. **GitHub "good first issue" — the first concrete step, not the whole plan.** *Fit:* the AI plan carries
    milestones; the hero should name only the next one and let the rest live in a fold. Build (2c).
15. **Superhuman — speed through keys.** *Fit:* ⌘K exists. Cut.
16. **Readwise — resurface what was highlighted.** *Fit:* what the professor stressed and called exam material is
    already captured from lectures. Put it on the hero when it touches the item's topic. Build, one line, only when
    real.
17. **Linear — "blocked by", and unblocking when the blocker closes.** *Fit:* gates exist and the gate outranks the
    gated; the human-blocker case (partner, feedback, instructor) is what is missing. Build (2a), with a plausible
    clear date and a "chase" line when the deadline closes in on a blocked item.
18. **Khan Academy — mastery by topic, not by class.** *Fit:* topic scores exist. On the hero: if the item's topic is
    one he has been shaky on, say so and point at the slides that cover it. Build, one line, only when real.
19. **Todoist — one primary action per task.** *Fit:* the hero has three buttons and a link; the brief wants "start."
    Build: Start (or Done once started), Open in Halo, and the rest folded.
20. **Amazing Marvin — every strategy is a toggle.** *Fit:* his 3d rule. Anything uncertain ships off by default.

## What is specific to him that none of them handle

21. **Stop opening Halo.** The one legitimate reason to open it is to submit. Put the direct Halo link on the hero so
    the app is the only place he looks until the moment of handing in. Build (2c).
22. **Overwhelmed by lists.** Drop the "then" list; the calendar exists. Build (2b). Everything on the hero is about
    one piece of work.
23. **Never had to study.** The gap is not motivation, it is not knowing how to begin. What it asks for, the first
    step, what full credit needs, the material that covers it, and a starter prompt for the tutor that walks him in
    without doing it for him. Build (2c, 2d).
24. **CLC group work.** Waiting on a partner is the commonest blocker in his week and reads as procrastination to
    every tool he has tried. Build (2a).
25. **LopesWrite.** A paper that goes through LopesWrite needs a day for it. Say so on the hero as a fact. Build.
26. **Voice Memos already transcribes.** Recording in the browser and uploading audio are both detours. Pasting the
    transcript is the path. Build (part 1), and make it the first thing on the class page.
27. **Loses interest and comes back later.** The pieces that survive a gap are the ones that need no memory of where
    he left off: the checklist with the next step called out, the timer that resets, the block that clears itself.
    Build into 2a and 2c rather than as a feature.

## The cut

Build: 1 (as principle), 3, 6/17/24 (blocked), 9, 10 (toggle, off), 12, 14, 16, 18, 19, 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, 27.

Cut: 2, 5, 8, 11, 15, 20 (as a feature; it is a rule), and anything that would show a second item on Now.

Kept in mind for the fresh-eyes pass: 4, 7, 13.
