## r/GCU (longer, with the honest framing)

**Title:** I'm a freshman and I built a planner that reads Halo announcements so you don't miss the "due Friday"
buried in one

**Body:**

> Six classes, six announcement feeds, and every week something graded shows up only in an announcement: "reply to
> two classmates by Sunday", "bring a printed copy", "the quiz covers chapters 1 and 2". I kept losing points to
> that, so I built Halo+.
>
> What it does:
> - One bookmark syncs your classes, assignments, grades and every announcement out of Halo. It runs on Halo's own
>   page while you're logged in, so it never asks for your password and never sees it.
> - Now: one screen, one card, the thing to do next, with the due date, how long it takes, what it's worth, and
>   the professor's own instructions from announcements quoted on it.
> - Grades from the gradebook, with what you need on the rest of the term.
> - Reminders as push notifications: the morning note, a heads-up the night before a heavy day.
> - Works on a phone from the Home Screen.
>
> It's independent, not from GCU, and I'm one student. The Halo sync and the announcement reading are Plus
> ($3.99 a month, cancel anytime); a Study tab that practices for your quizzes from your own slides and checks your
> work against the rubric is Max ($7.99). Every account starts with a week of Max free, no card, and the syllabus
> planner stays free. Your data is yours: export it or delete the account in one tap.
>
> https://richardsgeorger-collab.github.io/school-dashboard/
>
> I'd rather hear what's wrong with it than what's right. Which class of yours has the messiest announcements?
